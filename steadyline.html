Steadyline privacy policy

Steadyline changes how a page looks on your device. The text of the pages you read is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Settings you choose, such as toggles and the list of sites that turn on automatically, are stored with the browser storage API. If you are signed into the browser, the browser may sync those settings to your own account. We do not receive them.

On every install, including people who never pay, the browser contacts extensionpay.com with an anonymous install ID to check which plan is active. If you pay, ExtensionPay and Stripe also process your email and payment. We do not put page content in that request. If ExtensionPay cannot be reached, the extension stays on the free features.

Local PDF and EPUB files are opened on your device and are never uploaded.

The extension does not include analytics, advertising, or remote code.
